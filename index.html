Final Year Research

Benchmarking and Adapting Compact Multimodal Models for Sinhala Visual Question Answering

BSc (Hons) thesis, Robert Gordon University, 2026. Supervisors: Dr Ruvan Weerasinghe and Deshan Sumanathilaka.

  • Sinhala has no VQA dataset or benchmark for compact vision-language models.
  • Built a 37,318-pair Sinhala VQA dataset by translating a vocabulary-stratified subset of Visual Genome.
  • Benchmarked 7 compact VLMs zero-shot; adapted Gemma-3-4B-IT with QLoRA (BLEU 3.77 → 38.02).
  • Compared fine-tuning, continual pre-training and few-shot prompting; direct fine-tuning won.
